# Article Critique Platform

A comprehensive software platform for systematic journal article critique, designed for researchers and investigators in academic research.

## Features

- **13-Point Systematic Critique** covering:
  - Article title and topic relevance
  - Source and journal strength
  - PICO clarity in research title
  - Authors and affiliations assessment
  - Abstract quality (IMRAD format)
  - Introduction and background evaluation
  - Methodology suitability
  - Results validity
  - Data analysis appropriateness
  - Discussion quality
  - Conclusions justification
  - Research impact assessment
  - References appropriateness

- **Evidence-Based Guidelines**: Integrates CASP, STROBE, CONSORT, PRISMA, and SMART criteria
- **Multi-Format Support**: Upload PDF or DOCX files
- **Comprehensive Reports**: Generates detailed HTML and Markdown reports
- **Visual Scoring**: Color-coded scores with letter grades
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop and mobile devices
